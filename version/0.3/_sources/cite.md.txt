# Citing

If you find BLVPY useful in your research, please consider citing the following paper.

The original idea of **disciplined bilevel programming** was introduced in

```bibtex
@article{zhu2026dblp,
  title={Disciplined Bilevel Programming},
  author={H. Zhu and J. Boedecker},
  journal={arXiv},
  volume={2609.00644},
  year={2026}
}
```

Catalog links and additional materials are recorded on [this page](https://haozhu10015.github.io/papers/dblp.html).
